What Neap watches for you

Less chart reading, more water time.

One call, not twelve charts

Swell size and period, wind and tide are weighed for your break and boiled down to one word: flat, marginal, fun or firing.

Alerts that know your board

Tell Neap you ride a longboard and it stops waking you for overhead closeouts on the reef.

Tide tables that work offline

The week's tides and the last forecast stay on your phone for the car park with one bar of signal.
